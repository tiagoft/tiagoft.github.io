About

I am a researcher at Insper in São Paulo. I study how neural networks recover — or fail to recover — meaningful structure from human-generated data, using language, audio, and music as different surfaces of the same problem in representation learning.

I am always interested in connecting with researchers and engineers working on representation learning, ML systems, and privacy-preserving AI.
